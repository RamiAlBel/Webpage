Play Quirky2000

Quirky2000 is my chess engine: C++, alpha-beta search and a neural-network (NNUE) evaluation trained on Lichess games scored by Stockfish. Every idea in it was tested in engine-vs-engine matches before it was kept. The version below is compiled to WebAssembly and runs entirely in your browser, on one thread.

Press start.

Click a piece, then a highlighted square. Pawns promote to a queen. The engine and its 6 MB network load the first time you scroll here.
